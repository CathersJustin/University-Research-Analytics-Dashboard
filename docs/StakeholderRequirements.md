# Stakeholder Requirements

## Vice President for Research

### Primary Questions

- Is total research funding increasing?
- Which colleges are contributing the most research activity?
- Which sponsors are most important to the university?
- Where should additional research support be directed?

### Dashboard Needs

- Executive KPIs
- Year-over-year funding trends
- College comparisons
- Sponsor concentration
- Research area trends

## College Deans

### Primary Questions

- How is the college performing compared with other colleges?
- Which departments submit the most proposals?
- Which departments have the highest success rates?
- Which departments may need additional grant support?

### Dashboard Needs

- College and department filters
- Proposal volume
- Success rate
- Awarded funding
- Department rankings

## Department Chairs

### Primary Questions

- How many proposals has the department submitted?
- Which principal investigators are most active?
- Which sponsors fund the department?
- How does the department perform over time?

### Dashboard Needs

- Department-level metrics
- Principal investigator analysis
- Sponsor analysis
- Funding trends

## Research Administrators

### Primary Questions

- How many proposals are pending?
- How long do sponsor decisions take?
- Which sponsors create the greatest workload?
- Are there unusual delays or status patterns?

### Dashboard Needs

- Proposal status breakdown
- Average decision time
- Sponsor workload
- Proposal-level detail
- Date filters

## Data Analysts

### Primary Questions

- Are calculations defined consistently?
- Can the data be validated and reproduced?
- Are field definitions and transformations documented?
- Can reports be maintained over time?

### Dashboard Needs

- Data dictionary
- Calculation documentation
- SQL validation queries
- Python quality checks
- Metadata
- Data flow documentation
